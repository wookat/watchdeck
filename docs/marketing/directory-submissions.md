## Standard copy block

- **Name:** WatchDeck
- **URL:** https://watchdeck.zalize.com
- **Tagline (60 chars):** Web-first TV & movie tracker — import your TV Time data
- **Short description (140):** Track TV shows & movies on the web. Import your TV Time, Trakt, Serializd or Netflix history in one click and pick up your next episode.
- **Long description:** WatchDeck is a web-first TV show and movie tracker built for TV Time refugees. Upload your TV Time GDPR export ZIP (or Trakt/Serializd/Netflix CSVs) and WatchDeck matches every show, episode, movie and rating — then your home screen simply shows what to watch next. It adds an airing calendar with iCal feeds and email reminders, watch statistics with shareable cards, custom and public lists, streaming-service availability filters, episode-level rewatch tracking and a year-end Wrapped. Works in any browser on phone and desktop, no app to install. Free while in beta; full JSON/CSV export any time — your data stays yours.
