## Maker comment (first comment)
Hey hunters 👋

When TV Time closed in July, years of my watch history — every episode, every rating — was suddenly a ZIP file with nowhere to go. WatchDeck is the home I built for it.

Drop the GDPR export in as-is (Trakt, Serializd and Netflix CSVs work too) and about a minute later your home screen just shows the next episode of everything you watch. Around that: an airing calendar with iCal + email reminders, watch stats with shareable cards, lists you can publish, streaming-service filters, rewatch tracking, and a year-end Wrapped.

Choices we care about:
- **Web-first** — works on any device, nothing to install (the big mobile trackers have no web app).
- **Your data stays yours** — full JSON/CSV export and one-click account deletion, always free.
- **No ads, no tracking cookies** — first-party cookieless analytics only.

Everything is free during the beta (paid Plus comes later — beta users won't be silently charged). Would love your feedback, especially from fellow TV Time refugees: what's missing from your workflow?

## FAQ
- **Is it really free?** Every feature is free while in beta. A Plus plan ($1.99/mo · $19/yr) is announced for after the beta; nobody is charged during it.
- **What can I import?** TV Time GDPR ZIP, Trakt-style CSV, Serializd CSV, Netflix ViewingActivity.csv — with a confirmation step before anything is written.
- **Can I get my data out?** Yes — JSON and CSV export any time, account deletion in one click.
- **Where does metadata come from?** TMDB (not endorsed or certified by TMDB).
- **Mobile app?** It's a responsive web app that works great on phones; installable as a PWA.
